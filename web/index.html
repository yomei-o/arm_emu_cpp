arm_emu_cpp

A 32-bit ARM Linux emulator — A32 and Thumb-2 — compiled to WebAssembly. Everything below runs in this tab. Nothing is uploaded.

A freestanding ELF

Two builds of one source: one in ARM state, one in Thumb. They are different instruction sets encoding the same program.

busybox

Alpine's static armv7 busybox, Thumb-2 end to end — a binary that has never heard of this emulator. A 0.6 MB fetch.

CPython 3.12, dynamically linked

A stock armv7 build of CPython, with its own ld-musl-armhf.so.1 doing every relocation — so what runs is the real loader, not a stand-in. A 15 MB fetch, decompressed by the browser's own DecompressionStream.




…or drop your own 32-bit ARM ELF here.
